● Live Meeting Assist

Live captions, rough speaker turns, optional translation, and auto-generated minutes — all running in this browser tab.

Before you start: click "Start sharing audio" below, then in the picker choose the meeting's browser tab (best option) or a window/screen, and make sure you tick "Share tab audio" / "Share system audio". Without that checkbox ticked, the browser won't hand this page any sound to transcribe. Right after that, if "Also include my microphone" is on below, your browser will separately ask for microphone permission — allow it if you want your own side of the conversation transcribed too, not just what other participants say.

Capture

Sharing a tab/screen only captures what's being played OUT of it — i.e. the other participants, as your meeting app plays them back to you. It can't see your own voice at all, since that normally doesn't get looped back through your speakers. Leave this on to also grab your microphone and mix it in, so your side of the conversation gets transcribed too.
